# Venue interior photos, round 2 (scoreboard backgrounds)

These are freely licensed photos of the inside of each stadium, taken from Wikimedia Commons as full-width backgrounds for the football scoreboard. Each venue folder holds the original files, downloaded at full resolution, plus a `SOURCES.json` that gives each file's Commons page, original URL, size, license, author, a ready-to-use credit line, whether it is a day, night or indoor shot, and a note on where the field and crowd sit in the frame.

Rules applied: CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA only, checked against Commons `extmetadata.LicenseShortName`, with no NC/ND and no fair use. Each photo is an inside-the-bowl view of the current stadium, landscape and at least 1920 px wide, with no identifiable faces as the subject. For open-air stadiums the folder holds a day shot and a night shot where one exists. For domes and roofed stadiums it holds one indoor shot and a runner-up.

The pixel regions in the notes are estimates from thumbnails, good enough to choose a crop or `object-position`, not exact.

## Gaps and caveats

Every venue has a primary photo that meets the rules. These are the places where something fell short:

- **Rose Bowl, night.** No true after-dark photo with a crowd qualified. The night pick (2017 Rose Bowl Game) was taken at dusk with the stadium lights on and a twilight sky. There is no night runner-up. The 2024 Rose Bowl Game set on Commons is all close-up action, and the other night photos either show only the video board or are smaller than 1600 px (`Rose Bowl Stadium Interior.png`, 1786×854, is too short).
- **Rose Bowl, day.** Both day picks are 2048 px wide, which is above the 1920 minimum but has little room to crop on large screens.
- **State Farm Stadium.** No runner-up qualified. The other photos are exteriors, security or flyover shots, or 2006 construction photos at 1600 px.
- **Hard Rock Stadium, night.** The primary night photo is from a soccer match (El Clásico, 2017, after the canopy was added), so the field has soccer markings. The football night runner-up (Hurricanes, Nov 2017) has two spectators' heads, seen from behind, large in the foreground and needs a crop above them.
- **AT&T Stadium.** The primary photo is from 2015. The most recent interior on Commons (`AT&T Stadium Inside (Dec 2025).jpg`) was passed over because the stands were only partly full.
- **Lucas Oil Stadium.** The primary photo is a 16.6 MB PNG, the original upload. Convert it to JPEG or WebP before it ships.
- **Backs of heads.** A few photos have spectators' heads, seen from behind and unidentifiable, along the bottom edge. Each one is flagged in its `SOURCES.json` note.
