AVAILABILITY UPDATES
• Notre Dame Football will provide an availability reporting
structure in an effort to streamline communication
surrounding student-athlete availability.
• In each week’s Monday game notes, the program will share a
list of all student-athletes who have availability affected for
the week. They will be classified as out for the season, out for
that week’s game, or questionable for that week’s game, as
well as the general ailment. That listing will be included with
the depth chart.
• An updated availability report will be released on Thursday
prior to Coach Freeman's late week Zoom call.
• Before each game, Notre Dame will continue to release an
availability update roughly 60 minutes prior to kickoff that will
confirm or update the availability of any student-athlete listed
on that week’s depth chart.
• As the information will be readily available, Notre Dame
coaches and student-athletes will not comment on or provide
additional information on availability in press conferences or
interviews throughout the week.
