Turner's 74-yard TD run in the fourth quarter was the second-longest rush for a Tulane player
over the last four seasons, behind Lucas' 80-yarder.
Turner is the first Tulane player with three rushes of 40+ yards in same game in more than 20
years. The last American player to do so was Navy's Blake Horvath vs. Memphis in September
of 2024.
With the win, Tulane improved to 11-24 all-time against Southern Miss in a series that started
in 1979. Tulane has now won four of the last five contests against the Golden Eagles. The win
also secured Tulane the "Battle for the Bell" Trophy.
The win improved Tulane to 41-36 all-time against current schools in the Sun Belt Conference.
The Green Wave reached .500 after four games for the third time since 2020 (2026, 2024, and
2020), eighth time since 2010 (2026, 2024, 2020, 2017, 2015, 2013, 2011, and 2010) and the
38th time since the start of the program in 1893.
Coach Hall improved his all-time record to 3-0 all-time as a head coach at Yulman Stadium.
Tulane was one of only 13 FBS teams to not play an FCS team this season.
For their efforts, Turner was named American Conference Offensive Player of the Week, while
punter William Hudlow claimed his second Ray's 8 honor from the Ray Guy Award.
Game Notes at Kansas State
Cooper Helmke's 58-yard field goal was a school record and bettered Cairo Santos 's previous
mark of 57 set against Rice during the 2012 campaign (11/3/12). Additionally, it was the first
time that Helmke had attempted a field goal or a PAT in his career.
The 58-yard field goal was only the second time that a Tulane kicker has been successful on
a kick beyond 54 yards, but the 25th time a kick has been successful kick from 50+ in the
program's history. He is the 13th Tulane kicker to ever make a 50+ field goal (Cairo Santos
twice, Ed Murray twice, Mark Olivari twice, followed by Nick Beucher, Seth Marler (four times),
Tony Wood (three times), Don Bright, Patrick Durkin (four times), Brad Palazzo (twice), Wayne
Clements, Carl Woodward, and Ross Thevenot.
The last time that Tulane had a 50+ field goal was Patrick Durkin's last season against Charlotte
(52 yards – 11/29/25).
In the third quarter, Helmke made a 53-yard attempt to become the first Tulane kicker since
Durkin to make two or more 50+ kicks in a game (vs. Temple – 11/22/25). Olivari also did it
against Air Force on November 8, 1975.
With the loss, Tulane dropped to 2-2 all-time against Kansas State in a series that started in
1988. Three of the four games have happened over the last four seasons.
With the loss, Tulane is 40-61 all-time against current schools in the Big 12 Conference with the
majority of those games coming against former American Conference members Houston (27)
and Cincinnati (19). Tulane's first game against a current Big 12 Conference member came in
1908 against Baylor.
The Green Wave has a losing record after the first three games for the second time the last
three seasons (2024), the fifth time since 2016 and the 61st time since the start of the program
in 1893.
The Green Wave's six sacks allowed was its most since yielding seven to SMU in the 2023
American Conference title game, which was played inside Yulman Stadium.
Saturday was the first time that head coach Will Hall has ever faced a Big 12 opponent.
