GAME DAY AT A GLANCE 
• Saturday afternoon will mark the 40th meeting between Notre Dame and Stanford. The series stands at a 25-14-0 Notre Dame 
advantage all-time.
• The Irish are 3-1 against the Cardinal under Freeman.

• The Irish are 21-4 against current ACC opponents under Dick Corbett Head Coach Marcus Freeman. Freeman has five wins 
against ACC opponents in every season he has coached at Notre Dame.
• The two teams play for the Legends Trophy, which is a combination of Irish crystal and California redwood.
• The Notre Dame Club of the San Francisco area first presented the trophy in 1989.

NOTRE DAME STADIUM NOTES 
• Notre Dame Stadium has been the home of Irish football since 1930. The Irish are in their 98th season at Notre Dame Stadium 
in 2026.
• Notre Dame holds a 536-133-13 record at home all time.
• Seating capacity is listed as 77,622 while the playing surface is artificial field turf.
• All seven home games of the 2025 season were sold out: the 19th, 20th, 21st, 22nd, 23rd, 24th and 25th sell-outs of the 
Marcus Freeman era. The last 22 games in a row, dating back to the start of the 2023 season, have been sold out.  Stanford 
has already been announced as sold out, with another capacity crowd expected this Saturday afternoon. 
IRISH WEAR GREEN 
• Notre Dame is 7-1 in Irish Wear Green contests since 2018 and is 3-1 when wearing green jerseys in Irish Wear Green games.
• The Irish are 8-1 when wearing green jerseys since 2010.
• Most recently, Notre Dame was victorious over Boise State by a 28-7 score in a 2025 early-season matchup as the Irish donned 
green jerseys and green pants in the annual Irish Wear Green contest.
• This year's jerseys will feature the recently introduced football leprechaun logo on the shoulders. It is the first time a sport 
specific leprechaun will be showcased on the Notre Dame Football uniform. The last time a leprechaun logo appeared on the 
shoulders of a football uniform was on the Irish green 2015 Shamrock Series jerseys that were worn in Fenway Park against 
Boston College.
• The football leprechaun was the first in a series of sports-specific leprechauns unveiled by the University of Notre Dame 
Athletics Department in August of 2025. The mark was designed in-house by Fighting Irish Media, with input from the many 
around Notre Dame’s athletic programs and inspired by photos, fundamentals and running styles from former and current 
student-athletes.

defenders. Moore also has 22 career pass breakups, three forced fumbles

North Carolina. CJ Carr found Jordan Faison for a 28-yard score at 6:41

seconds apart against Stanford in 2018.

26-for-26 on extra points in 2026.

2021 season, the most in the FBS over that span. Drayk Bowen’s 15-yard

offensive touchdown of 2026.
