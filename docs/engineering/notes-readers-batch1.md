## How a name gets on screen

1. The school's notes page is read for this season's documents under the school's own label; the newest three are tried, newest first.
2. A document counts as the school's own only when its pronunciation guide names at least five players on ESPN's roster. That is what passes over the opponent's notes and depth charts filed beside it (Arizona's depth chart was passed over this way).
3. Each line is kept only when its name is on ESPN's roster, so a coach or an opponent never appears. The sound is shown as the school printed it.

## For your eye

- **Akron, two names lost.** The PDF's text comes out with doubled letters ("Aaamii", "Cravaack"), so they match no one on the roster and are left out, not guessed. Thirteen of fifteen are shown.
- **Coastal Carolina, three names lost.** Chandavian Booker, Obi Onwuka and Shai Thompson are in the school's guide; ESPN's roster does not list them under those names.
- **"Rhymes with" entries.** Troy prints "Rhymes with PORCH" (Mojo Dortch) and "Rhymes with lodge" (Rodge Waldrop). They show as "Say Rhymes with PORCH". Accurate, slightly odd. Left as the school wrote them.
- **Likely typos in the schools' own guides, shown as printed:** UMass "Dinos Drossos: Dra-roe-sos"; Coastal Carolina "Gavin McLaughlin: MIC-GOF-LEN".
- **Akron respells the whole name in capitals** ("SEBASTIAN BROUGHT-TON"). Shown as printed.
