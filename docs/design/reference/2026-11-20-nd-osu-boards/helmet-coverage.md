# Helmet photo coverage on Wikimedia Commons

Surveyed 2026-11-21. Research only: nothing was downloaded into the app. Covers every program in `teams/index.js` (138 teams).

## Summary

| Rating | Teams |
|---|---|
| A: sharp side/three-quarter profile, isolatable | 6 |
| B: usable with heavy cropping or an awkward angle | 11 |
| C: poor (small, hidden, partial or not current design) | 13 |
| none: no candidate | 108 |

**6 of 138 teams (4.3%) have an A-grade photo.** A or B: 17 (12.3%). 108 teams (78%) have nothing usable.

Conclusion: Commons cannot be the primary source for a helmet image per team. It covers a handful of programs well, and those images are inconsistent in angle, era and background, so they would not read as a set.

## Method

- For each team: Commons file search (namespace 6) for `<name> football helmet` and `<name> helmet`, plus the team's football category (`Category:<name> <nickname> football`) and any helmet or uniform subcategories.
- Filtered on `extmetadata.LicenseShortName` (CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA), width of at least about 800 px, and "helmet" in the title, description or categories. Military, historical-armour, NFL, bicycle and similar hits were excluded.
- Remaining helmet photos, plus the best game photo for teams with none, were checked visually from 500 px thumbnails and rated.
- Limits: game-action photos were only reviewed where the text mentioned a helmet or the title named the team, so a few more C-grade (and the odd B-grade) shots almost certainly exist uncatalogued in team categories. Ratings come from thumbnails, not full-resolution files. "Current style" is judged against the team's present helmet design.
- Re-check each file's license and attribution on its Commons page before use; CC BY and CC BY-SA need attribution, and CC BY-SA edits must be shared alike.
